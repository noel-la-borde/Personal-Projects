If you can dream it, you can make it happen.

Actaint Warriors Academy is an art academy that focuses on actuation and painting by separate or even combined.
Our mission is to give doors to success for those artists that are talented with a dream of becoming actors or painters in the future. Our variety of programs and special classes would make a roockie able to handle professional dares in the art industry.


Actaint W. was created by Noel La Borde , a Reading Area Community College Computer Technology student who loves art in all its fields and deep inside always wanted to be an actor.

We work and teach two different fields at the same time. Acting, which is the performing art in which envolves movement, and painting, which is the practive of applying paint, pigment, color or other medium to a solid surface called the "Matrix" or "Support".
