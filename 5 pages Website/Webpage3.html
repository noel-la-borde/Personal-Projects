Actaint Painting

Actaint Warriors Academy focuses on the following painting techniques:

  • Acrylic.
    • Acrylic paint is a fast-drying paint made of pigment suspended in acrylic polymer emulsion and plasticizers, silicone oils, defoamers, stabilizers, or metal sopus. Actaint Acrylic classes are water-based, these paints are used as latex house paints, as latex is the technical term for a suspension of polymer microparticles in water.

  • Canvas Oil Painting.

    • Canvas has become the most common support medium for oil painting, replacing wooden panels. Our Canvas Oil Painting classes are very basic at the beginning but then they get hard until students start approaching profesionals oil painting methods.
