Actaint Acting

Actaint Warriors Academy focuses on the following acting techniques:

  • Classical Acting.
    • Classical Acting is a type of acting based on the theories and systems of select classical actors including the expresion of the body, voice, imagnation, personalizing, improvisation, external stimuli, and script analysis.

  • The Meisner Technique.

    • The Meisner Technique is an approach to the actor to not focus on themselves and instead concentrate on the other actors in the innmediate environment.
